Put your photos here.

Recommended:
- JPG or WEBP, roughly 1200px on the longest side (keeps the site fast to load)
- Name them anything you like, e.g. `first-date.jpg`

Then reference them in `src/data/content.ts`:
  - `storyMoments[i].photo` → e.g. "/photos/first-date.jpg"
  - `photos[i].src` → e.g. "/photos/beach-day.jpg"

Until you add real files, the placeholders will just show as empty
frames — nothing breaks.
